# Names are the canonical crate name as it appears in Cargo.toml
# `[dependencies]` (with underscores, where applicable — Cargo
# treats `rust_decimal` and `rust-decimal` differently, only the
# former is the canonical name on the registry).

# Source: top crates.io download stats at compile time, with
# cherry-picks for known typosquat targets (rust_decimal,
# bigdecimal — the 2022 rustdecimal incident squatted the
# underscored form).
